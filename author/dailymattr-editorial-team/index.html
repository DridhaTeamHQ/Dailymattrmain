About the Team

The dailymattr Editorial Team decides what to cover, writes every headline and summary in our own words, and takes editorial responsibility for all content published on dailymattr. The team operates under Dridha Technologies Private Limited, Madhapur, Hyderabad, India.

02

How We Work

  • Research across the public record – we review several published reports and official releases, then write one original summary of the verifiable facts.
  • Accuracy and fact-checking – we verify facts against credible sources before publishing.
  • Human review of AI-assisted content – a human editor reviews and approves every story.
  • Corrections – we correct significant errors promptly and openly.
