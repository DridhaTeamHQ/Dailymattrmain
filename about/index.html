About Us

15 Oct 2026

News that matters. Nothing that doesn't.

On this page

1. The problem 2. How DailyMattr solves it 3. Who we're for 4. Our promise

We live in the most connected era in history, yet staying informed has never felt harder. Every day, thousands of headlines compete for a few minutes of our attention across feeds, apps, alerts and forwards.

India's news and information space is still largely owned by legacy media brands. The way we live, work and consume content has changed completely, but the way news is packaged has barely moved.

01

The problem

The result is a paradox: we have more information than ever, and understand less of it.

Social media is built to keep you scrolling, not to keep you informed, and doomscrolling has become a habit across every age group. News apps are cluttered, sensational and often opinion-led, giving you more than you need and less of what you actually want: clarity.

So a growing number of Indians are tuning out of the news altogether. Not because they don't care, but because there's no good way to stay informed without the noise.

An informed country needs facts, not takes.

02

How DailyMattr solves it

DailyMattr is a finishable daily briefing built for urban India. Every day, we bring you the 100 stories that matter, across business, tech, finance, policy, sport, culture and world affairs, in clean, swipeable cards you can actually get through. No clutter. No fluff. No opinions dressed up as news.

  • Finishable by design. We cap each day at 100 top stories, so you end informed, not hooked.
  • Facts first. Every story passes a fact-check layer and our built-in editorial standards.
  • Learn your way. Go deeper with original audio and video explainers, newsletters and our Instagram pages.
  • Built for habit. Streaks and reader ranks reward consistency, turning scroll time into time well spent.
03

Who we're for

Students, young professionals, senior leaders and the self-employed: anyone who wants to stay on top of the world without losing an hour to it.

04

Our promise

DailyMattr isn't here to tell you what to think. We're here to make sure you know what's happening, why it matters and what it means for you, in a few minutes a day.

Stay informed. Stay sharp. Stay on what matters.
